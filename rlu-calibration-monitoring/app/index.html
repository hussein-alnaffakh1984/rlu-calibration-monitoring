    Method and code: Hussein Ali Hussein Al-Naffakh, University of Alkafeel. Automated change-point detection in raw immunoassay signals identifies calibration changes: validation against analyser records and comparison with patient-based quality control (manuscript in preparation). For quality-management and research use. Confirm change points with the analyser’s calibration history, control material or retained samples before acting on patient results. With thanks to Yasser Al-Mousawi Laboratory, Al-Samawah, for its cooperation and support of this work.
